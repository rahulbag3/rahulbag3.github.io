    ///// 04 · About

    About

    I write software and finish it. Most of what is here is an instrument of some kind: something that measures a network, watches a process, or helps a machine explain what it just did.

    The range is deliberate. Android control planes in Kotlin. Windows utilities on WPF and WinUI. A Next.js design system with the colour maths written by hand — sRGB through OKLCH to tonal palettes, no library. Different surfaces, same habit: build the real thing, then let the interface state plainly what is going on.

    Two of the five repositories are deliberately unfinished, and their READMEs say so. They are included anyway, because a half-built instrument with an honest label is more useful than a polished one that overstates itself.
